Who We Are

We Power Unforgettable Experiences

Our Mission

AV Rentall exists to power unforgettable experiences. We supply the complete technical backbone of live events — from pre-production planning through on-site execution — so that every performance, presentation, and broadcast lands with maximum impact.

Our Approach

We combine high-grade inventory with seasoned technical crew and end-to-end project management. Whether it's an intimate corporate dinner or a large-scale outdoor festival, AV Rentall delivers precision-engineered solutions tailored to each event's unique requirements.

What We Do

End-to-End Technical Production

AV Rentall provides a comprehensive suite of services designed to handle every layer of event technology — from concept and design through to live delivery and pack-down. Our integrated approach eliminates the friction of managing multiple vendors.

Service

Audio Systems

Line arrays, subwoofers, stage monitoring, in-ear systems, and FOH engineering for venues of all sizes — indoors and outdoors.

Service

Visual & Display

LED walls, projection systems, video switching, IMAG, content playback, and broadcast-grade camera packages for live and streamed events.

Service

Lighting & Rigging

Intelligent lighting rigs, trussing, motors, and structural stage builds custom-designed for the aesthetic and safety demands of each production.

Equipment

Professional Audio

Top-tier line array systems, digital mixing consoles, stage monitoring, wireless microphone systems, and in-ear monitoring for artists and presenters at any scale.

Equipment

Video & LED

High-resolution LED panels, large-format projection, video processors, broadcast cameras, IMAG systems, and content management solutions for live and hybrid events.

Equipment

Staging & Structures

Modular staging platforms, trussing systems, rigging hardware, crowd barriers, and custom fabricated scenic elements engineered to meet production and safety specifications.

Every service is backed by experienced crew, pre-event technical walkthroughs, and on-call support throughout your event.
