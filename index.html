What We Do

End-to-End Technical Production

AV Rentall provides a comprehensive suite of services designed to handle every layer of event technology — from concept and design through to live delivery and pack-down. Our integrated approach eliminates the friction of managing multiple vendors.

Service

Audio Systems

Line arrays, subwoofers, stage monitoring, in-ear systems, and FOH engineering for venues of all sizes — indoors and outdoors.

Service

Visual & Display

LED walls, projection systems, video switching, IMAG, content playback, and broadcast-grade camera packages for live and streamed events.

Service

Lighting & Rigging

Intelligent lighting rigs, trussing, motors, and structural stage builds custom-designed for the aesthetic and safety demands of each production.

Equipment

Professional Audio

Top-tier line array systems, digital mixing consoles, stage monitoring, wireless microphone systems, and in-ear monitoring for artists and presenters at any scale.

Equipment

Video & LED

High-resolution LED panels, large-format projection, video processors, broadcast cameras, IMAG systems, and content management solutions for live and hybrid events.

Equipment

Staging & Structures

Modular staging platforms, trussing systems, rigging hardware, crowd barriers, and custom fabricated scenic elements engineered to meet production and safety specifications.

Every service is backed by experienced crew, pre-event technical walkthroughs, and on-call support throughout your event.

FAQ

Got Any Questions?

What does AV Rentall actually supply?

Audio, visual, lighting and staging — the complete technical backbone of an event. That covers line arrays and consoles, LED walls and projection, intelligent lighting rigs, trussing and modular staging, plus the crew to run all of it.

Can I hire equipment without a crew?

Yes. Everything is available as dry hire or as part of a fully crewed production package, depending on how much support you want on the day.

What size of event do you work on?

We scale to the production — from a 50-person boardroom briefing to a 10,000-capacity open-air concert. Our resources are sized precisely to your event's demands.

Do you handle staging and custom sets as well?

Yes. Our in-house staging and fabrication team designs and builds bespoke stage structures, set pieces, and scenic elements to production and safety specifications.

Where are you based?

Our warehouse and offices are at M109 The Hangar Zwartkop, 0157. Collections and returns are arranged in advance — talk to us first and we will confirm the details.

How do I get a price?

Send the enquiry form below or WhatsApp us with your date, venue and rough requirements. Send us a detailed inquiry and we'll get back to you within 24 hours.
