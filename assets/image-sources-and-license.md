## Current usage guidance

Unsplash’s official help center states that Unsplash images are free to use for most commercial, personal, and editorial projects and that permission or contributor credit is not required, although credit is appreciated. See the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) and the [official commercial-use FAQ](https://help.unsplash.com/en/articles/2612315-can-i-use-photos-from-unsplash-for-personal-or-commercial-projects).

For production use, open the individual image page and record the photographer and canonical image URL in the website’s internal asset register. Confirm that the final crop does not create an implied endorsement and check for visible trademarks, recognizable people, or recognizable private property. If any of those create a concern, replace the image with an original AV Rentall photograph or another cleared source.

## Attribution recommendation

Attribution is not required under the current Unsplash guidance, but a small optional footer credit can be added if desired: `Photography via Unsplash.` If naming photographers, use the individual photographer page and image URL rather than relying only on a search-results URL.
