## Brand and visual direction

Use a strongly monochrome visual system inspired by a premium technical production studio. The dominant colors are near black `#161616`, off white `#F7F7F7`, pure white `#FFFFFF`, mist gray `#EAEBEE`, cool steel `#D3DBE3`, slate blue `#516A80`, and deep slate `#273746`. Keep the design approximately 90% black/off-white with slate blue used only for icons, links, focus states, chart accents, and small interface details.

The design should feel modern, exact, editorial, confident, cinematic, and spacious. Use a geometric sans-serif such as Plus Jakarta Sans, Manrope, or Inter. Use bold, tight display headings; small uppercase section labels with letter spacing; comfortable body copy; thin rules; restrained borders; and generous whitespace. Do not use colorful gradients, loud neon colors, generic startup illustrations, rounded cartoon cards, excessive shadows, or glassmorphism.
